Delivery & Returns

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Delivery Coverage

  • Ore: Same day or next morning.
  • Ondo State: 1-2 business days.
  • South-West Nigeria: 2-3 business days.
  • Other States: 3-5 business days.

Delivery Charges

Delivery fees depend on your location and order size. Final fee is confirmed before order dispatch.

Order Confirmation

All orders are confirmed through WhatsApp or phone before shipment. Please provide a valid contact number and delivery address.

Returns Policy

  • Returns are accepted for damaged, incorrect, or incomplete items.
  • Report issues within 24 hours of receiving your order.
  • Products must remain sealed and in original condition where applicable.

Refunds & Replacements

After verification, we will process a replacement or refund based on the issue reported. Processing time may vary depending on your payment method.
